Customer Support Hours: Zepto customer support is available via in-app chat 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, given the time-sensitive nature of quick commerce deliveries. Average in-app chat response time is under 2 minutes, and complex issues that cannot be resolved in chat are escalated to a specialist who follows up by email within 24 hours. Phone support is available for order-related emergencies between 8 AM and midnight, while non-urgent queries can also be raised by email at any time.
